Antena Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Antena ("the app") is an offline-first mesh messenger. It sends messages directly between nearby phones over Bluetooth and, when devices share the same Wi-Fi network, over the local network. It is designed to work without internet access and without any Antena server.

Summary

  • We do not run servers that receive your data. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.
  • The app has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Your identity, channels, and messages are stored on your device.

Information the app handles

  • Profile and identity: the display name and profile details you choose, plus cryptographic keys the app generates on your device. Keys are kept in the device's secure storage.
  • Messages and channels: channels you create or join, and messages you send or receive, are stored locally on your device. Messages are encrypted and signed before they are sent.
  • Location (optional): if you choose to share your location or a map marker, it is sent as a message to other nearby devices in that channel. Android requires the Location permission for Bluetooth scanning on some versions; the app does not use it to track you.
  • Nearby devices: the app discovers other Antena devices over Bluetooth and the local Wi-Fi network so it can deliver messages.

How messages travel

Messages you send in a channel are delivered to nearby devices and may be relayed by other Antena devices to reach people further away. Relaying devices forward encrypted messages and cannot read them unless they are members of that channel. Public channels use a channel key that is shared with devices that discover the channel, so anyone who joins a public channel can read it. Do not share sensitive information in public channels.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth (scan, connect, advertise) and Nearby devices: to find and talk to nearby devices.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi devices / local network and multicast: to find devices on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • Location: required by Android on some versions for Bluetooth scanning, and used for the optional location sharing feature.
  • Photos/files (optional): only if you choose to attach a file to a message.

Data sharing and retention

We do not receive your data, so we do not share it. Data stays on your device and on the devices of people in channels you use. You can delete data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Messages already delivered to other people's devices are controlled by those devices.

Security

The app uses modern encryption and digital signatures to protect and authenticate messages. No system is perfectly secure, and offline mesh networks have inherent limits: nearby devices can observe that radio traffic exists.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Emergency use

Antena is a best-effort communication tool. It does not guarantee delivery and must not be relied on as your only means of contacting emergency services.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
